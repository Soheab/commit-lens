{TIMESTAMP:f}Long Date, Short Time
{TIMESTAMP:F}Full Date, Short Time
{TIMESTAMP:s}Short Date, Short Time
{TIMESTAMP:S}Short Date, Medium Time
{TIMESTAMP:R}Relative, same as no style
